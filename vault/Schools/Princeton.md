## Money
- **Application fee:** Automatic: choose the US military veterans and servicemembers fee waiver option on the application. Lower-income applicants who apply for aid also get a waiver.
- **Aid model:** Aid is need-based and Princeton aims to meet 100% of need. A clear official statement of need-blind transfer admission was not retrieved. Princeton aid can supplement or replace veterans benefits.
- **Aid forms and dates:** Princeton uses its own Princeton Financial Aid Application (PFAA) and does not require or accept the CSS Profile or IDOC. Transfer aid deadline March 9.
- **Veterans benefits:** Participates in Service to School VetLink, Warrior-Scholar Project and the Leadership Scholar Program. Yellow Ribbon coordination through financial aid.

## Study abroad
Study abroad is open to spring-term sophomores, juniors and fall-term seniors with a 3.0 or higher GPA the prior year, and most students go no earlier than spring of sophomore year. Princeton need-based aid carries to approved academic-year programs, but the Princeton Grant does not cover summer programs. Transfer-specific rules were not found: ask the Study Abroad Program.
