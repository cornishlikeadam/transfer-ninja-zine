## Money
- **Application fee:** Automatic for U.S. veterans and active duty: answer Yes to the military fee waiver question in the Program Materials section.
- **Aid model:** Yale admits without regard to ability to pay and meets 100% of demonstrated need for Eli Whitney students. EWSP students are treated as independent, so parental income is not counted.
- **Aid forms and dates:** Financial aid application due April 1. Materials due March 15 (one Yale page says March 18, so confirm).
- **Veterans benefits:** Yellow Ribbon participant. Yale's veteran and military student office handles benefit coordination.

## Study abroad
Eli Whitney students can study abroad for a year or term with approval from the program director and the Committee on the Year or Term Abroad, doing full-time university-level work. Yale aid carries abroad with a budget form, capped at two terms. After returning you must enroll in Yale College: the catalog says two terms and a study abroad page says one, so confirm.
