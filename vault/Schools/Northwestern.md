## Study abroad
Study abroad eligibility starts the summer after you finish your first year at Northwestern, and this applies to transfers too, with a 2.5 GPA. Need-based aid covers Northwestern, exchange and partner programs but not independent programs. Summer aid covers one summer. Study abroad credit is treated as transfer credit.
