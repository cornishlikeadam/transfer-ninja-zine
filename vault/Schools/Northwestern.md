## Money
- **Application fee:** Armed Forces or Veteran Fee Waiver, plus hardship waivers, requested through a Common App or Coalition profile question. The fee is $75 otherwise. Requesting a waiver does not disadvantage you.
- **Aid model:** Need-aware or need-blind status for transfers was not established. Verify with admissions.
- **Aid forms and dates:** FAFSA and CSS Profile are both required (Northwestern CSS code 1565). Your audited list shows April 1 for the application, April 15 for FAFSA and CSS, and April 20 for tax forms. A search result cited March 15 and the admissions table lists aid on April 1, so verify before relying on any date.
- **Veterans benefits:** Yellow Ribbon was not confirmed in this search: ask the aid office.

## Study abroad
Study abroad eligibility starts the summer after you finish your first year at Northwestern, and this applies to transfers too, with a 2.5 GPA. Need-based aid covers Northwestern, exchange and partner programs but not independent programs. Summer aid covers one summer. Study abroad credit is treated as transfer credit.
