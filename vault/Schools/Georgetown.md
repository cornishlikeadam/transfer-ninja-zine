## Money
- **Application fee:** Fee waiver requests are accepted from any applicant for whom the fee is a significant burden, using the NACAC or College Board form. The deadline does not move for waiver requests.
- **Aid model:** Need-based aid only. Georgetown meets 100% of demonstrated need for U.S. citizens and permanent residents. International transfers are not eligible for aid.
- **Aid forms and dates:** Submit both the FAFSA and the CSS Profile. Application and aid March 1, with the junior Public Policy or Environment and Sustainability priority date January 15. Decisions by June 1.
- **Veterans benefits:** Dossier lists Yellow Ribbon with a full tuition match in the College. Confirm on Georgetown's military and veterans page.

## Study abroad
All aid except Federal Work-Study applies to Georgetown-approved and independent petition study abroad programs, and students in Global Education programs stay matriculated. No study abroad while on probation. Summer aid needs 6 or more credits. Georgetown Qatar covers one semester. Transfer-specific rules were not found: ask the Office of Global Education.

## Supplements and guidance (6)

### Short answer: a different viewpoint
250 words. Required for all applicants

> Everyone deals with people who hold viewpoints different from their own. Describe one such experience and what you learned from it.

- Pick one real exchange with a named setting: a canvassing door, a floor conflict as mentor, or a debate in Ethics of Emerging Technology.
- The learning is the point. Spend about half the words on what changed in how you think or act, not on who was right.
- Show you can state the other person's view fairly in one sentence before you give yours.
- Avoid a political hot-button unless the lesson is about listening. Georgetown reads this as a test of civil disagreement.
- At 250 words there is room for one scene and one lesson. Cut background that does not serve either.
