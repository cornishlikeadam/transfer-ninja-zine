## Money
- **Application fee:** UChicago says it charges no application fee for veterans and their dependents in the College. Whether that covers transfer applications was not stated: confirm. Otherwise $75 unless you apply for need-based aid.
- **Aid model:** Transfer review is need-aware, but UChicago says it meets 100% of demonstrated need with a loan-free package for admitted transfers who applied for aid. Forms: the FAFSA (code 001774 per QuestBridge) and the UChicago Financial Aid Worksheet. The official veterans page says UChicago does not accept the CSS Profile.
- **Aid forms and dates:** March 1 for Transfer Early Decision and the opening rolling deadline (one third-party source). The aid worksheet date typically follows the application deadline.
- **Veterans benefits:** Participates in Yellow Ribbon with an unlimited number of awards across academic units.
- **Note:** Transfer Early Decision is binding: compare aid first.

## Study abroad
Transfers receive at least six quarters toward the 1800-unit residency rule, and an older catalog says work abroad may not count toward minimum residency. Transfers complete the Core and at least half the major at UChicago. Aid carries only for programs run by the University Study Abroad Office. Others require a leave and no UChicago aid. Students who matriculate in the summer get no institutional summer aid.
