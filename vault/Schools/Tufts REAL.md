# Tufts REAL

**Route:** REAL, for adults and veterans

> The one thing: Ask REAL how your credits count and whether you must study full-time.

## Facts
- Financial-aid materials are due in mid-April 2027. The official page does not publish the exact day.

## Next move
Ask about credits, full-time study and recent coursework. Request an aid estimate.

## Fit (the real degree path)
Arts and Sciences social-science or cognitive path: sociology, Science, Technology and Society, cognitive and brain science or economics. Computer science appears only as coursework, not the major. SMFA studio work needs written confirmation from REAL and SMFA.

## People and research to know (anchors, not contacts)
- **Elaine Schaertl Short.** Disability-centered, human-in-the-loop machine learning and assistive robotics.
- **Matthias Scheutz.** Directs human-robot interaction research; robot ethics.

## Interest
Tufts says it records legitimate engagement such as official visits, email or phone contact and virtual events. No REAL-specific weight is published. The supplement asks how you have engaged with Tufts, so only true engagement counts.

**Question to ask:** Which syllabi should I supply to separate credit for my intended Arts and Sciences major from general electives, and can REAL students use SMFA studio courses?

## Money
- **Application fee:** No automatic waiver is stated on the REAL page: contact REAL if the fee is a hardship. Tufts Engineering lists an $85 waiver for military and veterans, so confirm for REAL.
- **Aid model:** Need-based aid for eligible REAL students, meeting 100% of demonstrated need. Need-blind status was not confirmed. File the Tufts Financial Aid Form as well as the FAFSA. If you do not apply for aid now, you are not eligible for institutional aid in later years.
- **Aid forms and dates:** Application due March 15, 2027. Aid materials are due mid-April, with the exact day unpublished. Say on the REAL application that you intend to use VA benefits.
- **Veterans benefits:** Yellow Ribbon: $5,000 a year from Tufts, matched by the VA, for veterans at 100% Post-9/11 eligibility.

## Study abroad
Aid transfers automatically for Tufts-run programs. For external programs only federal and state aid and loans transfer, through a Consortium Agreement, and Tufts grants do not. Courses need department approval. Whether REAL students can use study abroad was not found: ask REAL.

## Steps from the research (written July 2026)
1. Confirm REAL eligibility and the correct school/degree route with real.admissions@tufts.edu; do not submit both REAL and conventional transfer applications.
2. Ask two recent academic instructors for recommendations and reserve any military/professional letter as an evidence-based addition.
3. Build a credit worksheet for the Computer Science AI focus, STS, cognitive science, and any desired SMFA studio courses; send Tufts the required course descriptions and keep full syllabi ready.
4. Attend one official REAL/transfer event and one public STS seminar when the Fall 2026 schedule posts; use each to answer a real question, not accumulate interactions.
5. Submit the Common App by March 15, 2027, any required art portfolio by March 22, the conventional Mid-Term Report by April 5 if applicable, and REAL aid materials by mid-April.
6. Obtain a written credit, time-to-degree, need-aid, GI Bill, and Yellow Ribbon estimate before enrollment.
