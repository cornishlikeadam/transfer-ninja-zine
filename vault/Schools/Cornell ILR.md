# Cornell ILR

**Route:** ILR external transfer

> The one thing: Get ILR to review your course titles, descriptions and grades, and confirm sophomore or junior entry.

## Facts
- ILR says most admitted transfers have a 3.4+ college average.
- Cornell as a whole admitted 9.3% of Fall 2024 transfer applicants. That is not an ILR rate.

## Next move
Map writing, economics, math and science courses with ILR. Choose next courses for ILR readiness and your real direction.

## Money
- **Application fee:** No transfer fee-waiver rule was found. Ask ILR admissions.
- **Aid model:** ILR says transfer aid follows the same need-based policies as first-year students. Whether domestic transfer admission is need-blind was not confirmed.
- **Aid forms and dates:** ILR admits transfers for fall only, with a March 15 application deadline. The CSS Profile transfer deadline was March 15 in the 2026 cycle.
- **Veterans benefits:** Yellow Ribbon participant with a Veterans Summer Bridge and a veteran admissions pathway that can waive some documents.

## Study abroad
ILR says transfer students are eligible to apply for semester study abroad and ILR credit internship programs (Ireland, England, Australia). The usual rules: full-time, junior or senior year, good standing and the ILR core finished. Aid transfers for Office of Global Learning programs and is revised for the term abroad. Meet the ILR study abroad advisor to check how transfer status counts.

## Steps from the research (written July 2026)
1. Choose the Cornell undergraduate college first and audit every college-specific prerequisite against SCAD transcripts and syllabi.
2. By early fall, ask a recent SCAD instructor who graded analytical or technical work for the required academic recommendation.
3. Attend one official transfer/college event and ask the transfer-credit or preparation question above if the published materials do not resolve it.
4. Build the essay around one Applied AI project, its human consequences, and a specific Cornell method, concentration, lab, or course sequence.
5. Prepare the Common App, Cornell supplement, College Report, Mid-Term Report, transcripts, aid forms, and any permitted portfolio well before the standing March 15 deadline.
6. Do not automate email opens/clicks, mass-contact faculty, mail promotional packages, or seek a recommendation from someone who knows the applicant only through a recent cold email.
