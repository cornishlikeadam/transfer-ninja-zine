# USC Iovine and Young

**Route:** Iovine and Young Academy

> The one thing: Calculate the 3 to 4 year time and cost, and verify MATH 118 or 125 equivalency, before investing.

## Facts
- USC overall transfer rate was 26.1% in the Fall 2026 profile, with admitted GPAs of 3.70 to 3.97. Those are not Academy figures.
- Academy essays go in SlideRoom, with up to four optional portfolio pieces.

## Next move
Work out the realistic finish date and aid. If you apply, submit the required Academy essays and choose optional portfolio items.

## Fit (the real degree path)
Iovine and Young Academy BS. Dornsife BA Cognitive Science and Annenberg are the liberal-arts alternates. The sequential curriculum can mean 3 to 4 years.

## People and research to know (anchors, not contacts)
- **Kate Crawford.** Annenberg; the power, labor and politics of AI; Anatomy of an AI System.
- **Bistra Dilkina.** Co-directs the Center for AI in Society.

## Interest
USC states directly that it does not track demonstrated interest. Event attendance and emails add no points.

**Question to ask:** Which Academy requirements are non-waivable for an external transfer, and should I budget three or four USC years?

## Money
- **Application fee:** Veteran or service member waiver: select it under the school-specific question in the USC Questions section of the Common App. Hardship waivers are also available.
- **Aid model:** USC describes itself as need-blind for admission. Veterans should file the FAFSA and CSS Profile even when planning to use VA benefits, and can choose the GI Bill after seeing the aid package.
- **Aid forms and dates:** Your audited list shows February 15 for the application and March 2 for FAFSA and CSS priority filing. Other USC pages cite earlier dates, so verify.
- **Veterans benefits:** Yellow Ribbon participant with an unlimited undergraduate program for eligible students. USC says eligible undergraduates with remaining GI Bill can graduate debt-free.

## Study abroad
Iovine and Young BS students can fulfil up to 12 major units abroad with faculty approval, limited to major electives, with spring of junior year preferred. Whether aid applies to an Academy study abroad term was not found: ask. Outside-transfer eligibility for the Academy also needs written confirmation.

## Steps from the research (written July 2026)
1. By August, choose among Iovine and Young, Annenberg/Dornsife, and Viterbi based on prerequisites, transfer credit, and remaining semesters—not prestige.
2. Use USC’s transfer planning worksheet and articulation resources, then ask Iovine and Young the sequencing question above if that is the chosen route.
3. Build one portfolio case study around problem framing, contextual research, prototype, human-in-loop decisions, testing, and measurable learning.
4. Obtain an academic recommendation only if the target school/program requests or permits it; do not substitute an alumnus or faculty cold contact.
5. File the Common App after August 1 and before the correct December 1 or February 15 deadline; complete aid by March 2, 2027.
6. Secure a written VA/Yellow Ribbon and time-to-degree estimate before committing, especially for a three- or four-year Academy sequence.
