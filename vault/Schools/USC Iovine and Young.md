## Money
- **Application fee:** Veteran or service member waiver: select it under the school-specific question in the USC Questions section of the Common App. Hardship waivers are also available.
- **Aid model:** USC describes itself as need-blind for admission. Veterans should file the FAFSA and CSS Profile even when planning to use VA benefits, and can choose the GI Bill after seeing the aid package.
- **Aid forms and dates:** Your audited list shows February 15 for the application and March 2 for FAFSA and CSS priority filing. Other USC pages cite earlier dates, so verify.
- **Veterans benefits:** Yellow Ribbon participant with an unlimited undergraduate program for eligible students. USC says eligible undergraduates with remaining GI Bill can graduate debt-free.

## Study abroad
Iovine and Young BS students can fulfil up to 12 major units abroad with faculty approval, limited to major electives, with spring of junior year preferred. Whether aid applies to an Academy study abroad term was not found: ask. Outside-transfer eligibility for the Academy also needs written confirmation.
