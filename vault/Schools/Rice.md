# Rice

**Route:** Transfer Program

> The one thing: Meet the 3.2 minimum recent-college GPA and plan for four full-time semesters at Rice.

## Facts
- Rice admitted 102 of 1,473 transfer applicants in 2024-25 (6.9%). Admitted transfers typically have a 3.5 or higher GPA.
- Needs two college-instructor recommendations and the College Report. There are no transfer interviews.
- Rice asks transfers to explain their specific interest, so write it precisely.
- Rice specifies a 3.2 minimum GPA in the most recent college work; meeting it does not guarantee admission.

## Money
- **Application fee:** $75 fee, with a waiver for domestic applicants and a Rice-specific waiver. A veteran-specific waiver was not found: ask admissions.
- **Aid model:** Rice uses the CSS Profile (school code 6609). Need-blind status for transfers was not confirmed, and secondary sources say international transfers get no institutional aid.
- **Aid forms and dates:** Application due March 15 (Fall 2027 not republished). The aid office lists April 15 for transfer aid applications.
- **Veterans benefits:** Your dossier lists the Rice Military Veterans association, VetLink and Yellow Ribbon support. Confirm with the VA certifying official.
- **Note:** Transfers complete at least four semesters (60 semester hours) full-time at Rice.

## Study abroad
Arrange study abroad through the Rice Study Abroad Office to secure credit transfer and financial aid portability. Study abroad credit follows the transfer credit rule (C- or better). No transfer-specific aid rule was found: ask the Study Abroad Office and Financial Aid.
