## Money
- **Application fee:** $50 application fee or a fee waiver for qualified students, through the Common App or Coalition transfer application. Vanderbilt issues no CSS fee-waiver codes: the CSS Profile may waive its own fee based on your answers.
- **Aid model:** A secondary source says Vanderbilt meets 100% of demonstrated need for admitted transfers. Verify on Vanderbilt's own site.
- **Aid forms and dates:** Your audited list shows February 1 for FAFSA and CSS priority and February 15 for the priority application.
- **Veterans benefits:** The registrar certifies VA benefits. Yellow Ribbon contributions vary by school, so get a written estimate from the VA School Certifying Official.

## Study abroad
Transfer students can apply to study abroad after completing their first semester at Vanderbilt per the policies page, though another source says two semesters, so reconcile with the Study Abroad office. Semester and academic-year programs through Vanderbilt are eligible for federal and Vanderbilt aid, and the package can increase if a program costs more than a comparable term. Summer aid is limited.
