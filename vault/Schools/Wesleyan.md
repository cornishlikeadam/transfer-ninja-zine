## Money
- **Application fee:** The application fee is automatically waived for any student applying for financial aid.
- **Aid model:** Meets 100% of demonstrated need with loan-free packages for domestic students, but is need-aware for transfer admission. International transfers get very limited aid.
- **Aid forms and dates:** Aid request due March 15. If you say you will not seek aid, you are not eligible for Wesleyan aid until after one year (two full semesters) enrolled.
- **Veterans benefits:** Veterans benefits and Yellow Ribbon were not researched: ask financial aid.

## Study abroad
Semester and year study abroad for credit counts as Wesleyan enrollment, so Wesleyan tuition is charged and aid applies. The Office of Study Abroad prepares a budget sheet and aid is revised from it. Work-study is replaced by a grant. Wesleyan aid does not cover summer or winter programs. Transfer-specific rules were not found: ask the Office of Study Abroad.
