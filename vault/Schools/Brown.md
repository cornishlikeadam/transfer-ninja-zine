## Money
- **Application fee:** No application fee for veterans, who use the separate Veterans Application.
- **Aid model:** Veterans are admitted need-blind. General transfer admission is need-aware. Brown meets full demonstrated need.
- **Aid forms and dates:** Veterans submit the FAFSA, a prior-year tax return and a VA Certificate of Eligibility. Veteran aid materials were due March 1 in the 2026 cycle, so recheck 2027. On your call: VR&E pays out like a Post-9/11 GI Bill.
- **Veterans benefits:** Service to School VetLink partner with an unlimited undergraduate Yellow Ribbon program for eligible students.
- **Note:** The binding rolling option means you withdraw other applications if admitted. Compare aid first.

## Study abroad
Brown students in good standing can study abroad for a semester or year in sophomore, junior or senior year, after declaring a concentration. Brown aid can apply if aid requirements are met and a Consortium Agreement is filed for non-Brown programs. Courses need pre-approval to earn credit. No rule specific to transfer or veteran-route entrants was found: ask Brown study abroad advising.

## Steps from the research (written July 2026)
1. Use the Brown Veterans Application, as Brown explicitly directs veterans to do; do not submit RUE or the Common App in parallel.
2. Complete Service to School/VetLink early enough to develop the addendum and application strategy before Brown’s August opening.
3. Request an academic recommendation now and a service/professional recommendation from someone with direct, sustained knowledge.
4. Compare binding and nonbinding Veterans Application review only after running Brown’s aid estimate and understanding the withdrawal obligation.
5. Map SCAD syllabi to Brown’s course-credit system and use the credit question above if the public guidance cannot resolve the ambiguity.
6. Complete the optional video if it adds character and reflection; do not turn it into an advertisement or repeat the written application.
