## Money
- **Application fee:** The $65 fee can be waived, and Williams says asking does not disadvantage you. Veterans can request a waiver on the Common App. The CSS Profile fee can also be waived, and the $200 enrollment deposit too.
- **Aid model:** Williams offers only need-based aid with no merit scholarships. Need-blind status for transfers was not stated. The CSS Profile is required for non-federal aid.
- **Aid forms and dates:** Admission and aid applications are due together, with no extra materials for transfer aid. The official date is unclear (March 1 to 3).
- **Veterans benefits:** Williams encourages veterans to apply, as first-year or transfer depending on college coursework. Service to School VetLink guidebook has a Williams page.

## Study abroad
Williams requires two years in residence and caps transfer credit at two years. A student who enrolls as a junior and wants to study away must give up a semester or year of transfer credit to meet residence. Aid recipients submit a budget proposal for study away. A new tuition model removes a $1,500 study away fee from the Class of 2029, except for Williams-Exeter and Williams-Mystic. Whether transfers can join Williams-Exeter was not stated.
