## Money
- **Application fee:** Veterans get an application fee waiver: request it in the fee section of the transfer application, and it does not disadvantage you. Third-party sites put the fee at $90.
- **Aid model:** The official aid page lists the CSS Profile, FAFSA and IDOC for transfers, all due March 1. Need-blind status for transfers was not confirmed. One advice site says transfers get no Dartmouth aid while the official page implies they are considered, so confirm with financial aid.
- **Aid forms and dates:** March 1 for the application and the aid forms.
- **Veterans benefits:** Participates in Yellow Ribbon with no limit on the number of students, and counts veterans benefits as a resource when determining your need. Service to School partner.
- **Note:** Testing is required. A veteran may request a waiver if scores are old, never taken, or retesting is a hardship.

## Study abroad
Aid applies to Dartmouth-sponsored off-campus programs and official exchanges, adjusted to the actual cost. Financial aid is not available for transfer terms on non-Dartmouth programs, though loans may be possible. Transfer students are assigned a D-Plan after credit evaluation and need at least six enrolled terms.
