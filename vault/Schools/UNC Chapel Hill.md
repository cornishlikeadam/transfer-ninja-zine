## Money
- **Application fee:** $85 fee. Veterans and active-duty members can get a waiver (veterans since fall 2024): complete the fee waiver request form and email it to uncsubmit@admissions.unc.edu.
- **Aid model:** Aid forms are the FAFSA and CSS Profile. Transfers are evaluated automatically for the Carolina Covenant if the March 1 priority aid deadline is met. UNC has no published need-blind statement for transfers.
- **Aid forms and dates:** Transfer application February 15. Aid forms March 1 priority per your research. The official FAFSA date was not confirmed.
- **Veterans benefits:** Yellow Ribbon participant. Veterans using Post-9/11 or Chapter 30 benefits are granted in-state tuition by law. If benefits run out before you graduate, you must establish North Carolina residency to keep in-state rates.
- **Note:** In-state versus out-of-state cost differs enormously. Get a written net-cost estimate.

## Study abroad
Transfer students may study abroad after their first semester at UNC. Regular aid, except work-study, carries to approved programs, and the package can shrink if the program costs less. Email fa_studyabroad@unc.edu once you choose a program. Exchange and visiting students get no UNC aid.
