# SMU

**Route:** Transfer, academic unit to select

> The one thing: Apply only if a scholarship plus benefits makes SMU close to free. Get a written net-cost estimate first.

**Gate:** Stays on the list only with a full, decent scholarship. Automatic transfer merit needs 24 transferable hours and a 3.3 GPA, and the larger awards need 3.5 or higher.

## Facts
- The admission-balance option: 1,190 of 1,542 Fall 2025 transfer applicants were admitted (77.2%).
- Automatic transfer merit consideration starts at a 3.3 GPA.

## Money
- **Application fee:** The veterans page says SMU automatically waives the application fee for all undergraduate U.S. military veteran applicants. A newer sheet says all undergraduates this cycle, so confirm.
- **Aid model:** Merit comes automatically with 24 transferable hours and a 3.30 GPA: the Founders Scholarship is $15,000 a year for up to six terms, the Transfer Honor Scholarship is half tuition at 3.5, and a full-tuition-and-fees award needs 3.7 (North Texas community college students). Your gate is a full, decent scholarship.
- **Aid forms and dates:** FAFSA and CSS priority February 1. Priority application April 1. Final application August 1.
- **Veterans benefits:** Yellow Ribbon guarantees a 100% tuition benefit for eligible Post-9/11 veterans for the length of their entitlement. Veterans apply as transfer students.
- **Note:** Ask for a written net-cost estimate, with scholarship and benefits combined, before applying.

## Steps from the research (written July 2026)
1. Request a transfer-credit and Creative Computing requirement map using SCAD transcripts and full syllabi.
2. Use the April 1 fall priority date, not the August 1 final deadline, as the planning target for scholarship and schedule flexibility.
3. Attend a Transfer Friday or official transfer event and ask the major-credit question only if it remains unresolved.
4. Prepare a concise portfolio of two to four works with role, method, result, and reflection; do not send unrequested physical materials.
5. Confirm Yellow Ribbon, remaining cost, and VA-certification timing with SMU before making a binding financial decision.
6. Keep a strong academic and financial alternative even though the recent university-wide transfer admit rate is high.
