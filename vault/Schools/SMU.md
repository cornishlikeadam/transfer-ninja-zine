## Money
- **Application fee:** The veterans page says SMU automatically waives the application fee for all undergraduate U.S. military veteran applicants. A newer sheet says all undergraduates this cycle, so confirm.
- **Aid model:** Merit comes automatically with 24 transferable hours and a 3.30 GPA: the Founders Scholarship is $15,000 a year for up to six terms, the Transfer Honor Scholarship is half tuition at 3.5, and a full-tuition-and-fees award needs 3.7 (North Texas community college students). Your gate is a full, decent scholarship.
- **Aid forms and dates:** FAFSA and CSS priority February 1. Priority application April 1. Final application August 1.
- **Veterans benefits:** Yellow Ribbon guarantees a 100% tuition benefit for eligible Post-9/11 veterans for the length of their entitlement. Veterans apply as transfer students.
- **Note:** Ask for a written net-cost estimate, with scholarship and benefits combined, before applying.
