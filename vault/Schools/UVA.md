- **Application fee:** Sources conflict: a $75 fee waived for students eligible for College Board waivers, or a $60 fee. A veteran-specific waiver was not found. Ask admissions.
- **Aid model:** UVA requires the FAFSA and CSS Profile for need-based aid. Need-blind status for transfers, especially out-of-state, was not confirmed.
- **Aid forms and dates:** Fall transfer deadline March 1 with decisions by May 1. The aid date found, March 1, is a first-year date, so confirm the transfer aid date.
- **Veterans benefits:** Yellow Ribbon participant, with funds granted first come, first served. Your dossier lists a full in-state rate match for qualified veterans: confirm.
- **Note:** Residency changes the odds and the cost sharply. Ask how in-state treatment works for veterans.

## Study abroad
The College of Arts and Sciences requires a 2.5 cumulative GPA for study abroad, and transfer credit depends on content, level and comparability. UVA cannot pay a program directly. Need-based awards for out-of-state students can be lower for a term abroad. No transfer-specific rule was found: ask Student Financial Services.
