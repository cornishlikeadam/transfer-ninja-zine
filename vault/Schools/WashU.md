## Money
- **Application fee:** The $75 fee is waived for U.S. veterans applying for first-year or transfer admission: indicate eligibility on the Common App or Coalition App. It is also waived for applicants applying for need-based aid.
- **Aid model:** International transfer applicants are not eligible for aid. Need-blind status for domestic transfers was not confirmed.
- **Aid forms and dates:** Application and aid working date March 1, 2027. Verify when WashU relabels the Fall 2027 calendar.
- **Veterans benefits:** Yellow Ribbon was not confirmed in this search: ask financial aid.

## Study abroad
Need-based aid and scholarships can apply to approved WashU study abroad programs, but generally not to summer programs. A 3.0 GPA is required, and a C or better for credit. Transfer-specific rules were not found: ask Overseas Programs.

### Personal statement
Up to 650 words, 3,250 characters. Required; choose one Common App prompt

> Choose one of the seven standard prompts (background or identity, a challenge, a belief you questioned, gratitude, a period of growth, an engaging idea, or your own topic) and write on it.

- Pick the prompt after choosing the story, not before.
- The same statement can serve UNC, Rice and UChicago, which use the same prompt list. Write it once and write it well.
- A challenge or growth prompt fits your return to college. An engaging-idea prompt fits your ownership-of-AI argument.
- Use one scene, one turning point and one changed understanding.
- WashU says 650 words is a limit, not a goal. Stop when the story is told.
