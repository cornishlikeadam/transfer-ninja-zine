## Money
- **Application fee:** No transfer fee-waiver rule was found. Ask admissions.
- **Aid model:** A VetLink guide lists Notre Dame as need-blind for all admitted students. An official statement for transfers was not found. Transfer students are not eligible for merit scholarships.
- **Aid forms and dates:** From your call: a household income of $60,000 or less covers tuition, fees, housing and food, and up to $150,000 covers at least full tuition. You do not have to use the GI Bill to attend. Fall transfer deadline March 15.
- **Veterans benefits:** Yellow Ribbon school. On your call, a 100% GI Bill pays up to about $30,000 a year more or less. Service to School VetLink, Warrior-Scholar Project and a veterans law clinic.

## Study abroad
Students in approved programs count as enrolled at Notre Dame for federal aid. Aid is not guaranteed abroad, and non-Notre Dame programs need a Consortium Agreement. Because Notre Dame meets need, outside funds can adjust your aid. Same fees as campus plus a $750 study abroad fee. No rule specific to transfer entrants was found: ask Global Study Abroad.

## Steps from the research (written July 2026)
1. Choose an Arts and Letters program and an alternate only after checking each program’s required transfer courses and available space.
2. Download and rename a PDF syllabus for every post-high-school course now; Notre Dame requires all of them and will not accept course descriptions as substitutes.
3. Apply to Service to School/VetLink and contact Montanna Kirven or OMVA once with the veteran-credit/documentation question above.
4. Complete a conventional writing-intensive, quantitative-reasoning, or science course if the SCAD record does not clearly cover Notre Dame’s Core preparation.
5. Request the optional recommendation from a recent academic instructor; use Glimpse only if it adds character and clear spoken reflection.
6. Build the transfer statement around technology ethics and the common good, then submit by October 1, 2026 for Spring 2027 or March 15, 2027 for Fall 2027.
