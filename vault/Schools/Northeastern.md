# Northeastern

**Route:** College-specific Transfer

> The one thing: Get a preliminary credit map and co-op timeline before you decide it is affordable.

## Facts
- Fall transfer applications are due April 15 with rolling decisions by June 30.
- You count as a transfer after 12 semester hours or one attempted full-time college semester.
- The Northeastern Promise guarantees eight semesters of funding for students on need-based aid.

## Money
- **Application fee:** $75 fee. Fee waiver requests go through the Application Status Check. The Boston public high school waiver appears to be first-year only. A veteran waiver was not found.
- **Aid model:** The CSS Profile is required for need-based institutional funding, including for transfers (school code 3667). Fall transfer priority date is April 15. Federal-aid-only applicants skip the CSS.
- **Aid forms and dates:** April 15 priority for aid and application. Older sources list April 1, so confirm.
- **Veterans benefits:** CAVS coordinates support, and eligible 100% Post-9/11 undergraduates are automatically in the unlimited Yellow Ribbon program (your research).
- **Note:** Co-op timing and the credit map change time to degree and cost. Get both in writing.

## Study abroad
Traditional transfer students can join summer, semester or Dialogue of Civilizations programs after two matriculated semesters at Northeastern, and must be enrolled or on co-op the term before. Need-based aid applies to eligible programs with a FAFSA on file. Verify which summer terms qualify for aid.

## Steps from the research (written July 2026)
1. Compare AI and Philosophy with Computer Science and Design course by course, including co-op prerequisites and anticipated transfer credit.
2. Attend one official transfer information session; use it to clarify credit, campus, and co-op sequencing rather than to create an attendance count.
3. Ask the combined-major/first-co-op question once if the catalog and transfer session leave it unresolved.
4. Build one portfolio case study that connects design craft, AI implementation, responsible evaluation, and user impact.
5. Submit early enough for a complete rolling review, but do not confuse early completion with a binding or separately advantaged transfer round.
6. Before enrollment, obtain a written cost and VA-benefit estimate from CAVS and financial aid.
