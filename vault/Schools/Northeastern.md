## Money
- **Application fee:** $75 fee. Fee waiver requests go through the Application Status Check. The Boston public high school waiver appears to be first-year only. A veteran waiver was not found.
- **Aid model:** The CSS Profile is required for need-based institutional funding, including for transfers (school code 3667). Fall transfer priority date is April 15. Federal-aid-only applicants skip the CSS.
- **Aid forms and dates:** April 15 priority for aid and application. Older sources list April 1, so confirm.
- **Veterans benefits:** CAVS coordinates support, and eligible 100% Post-9/11 undergraduates are automatically in the unlimited Yellow Ribbon program (your research).
- **Note:** Co-op timing and the credit map change time to degree and cost. Get both in writing.

## Study abroad
Traditional transfer students can join summer, semester or Dialogue of Civilizations programs after two matriculated semesters at Northeastern, and must be enrolled or on co-op the term before. Need-based aid applies to eligible programs with a FAFSA on file. Verify which summer terms qualify for aid.
