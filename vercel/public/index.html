Tutor board

Fit, interest, proof

One row per school on the main list: the real degree path, the research worth knowing, whether interest counts, who to ask and what to ask, and how far the RECAP proof test has come. Open a card in the lineup for the full brief and to fill in the proof.

RECAP test

  1. Resource. Name a real course, lab, dataset, paper, public initiative or cross-school mechanism.
  2. Evidence. State what it actually studied, built or found, with the primary source.
  3. Connection. Say which part of your own work gives you a reason to care.
  4. Application. Propose a feasible undergraduate action.
  5. Proof. Show what you already did because of the research.

The order that counts

  1. The correct route and a complete file
  2. Academic readiness and a clear transfer reason
  3. School-specific essays that show tested fit
  4. Any required interview, portfolio or supplement
  5. One official session and one precise question, when needed
  6. A campus visit, if it improves the decision
  7. Everything else only if it is useful on its own

What earns nothing

  • Email opens, link clicks, newsletters and repeat browsing
  • Mass emails to faculty, or a faculty name you have not read
  • Mailed letters, gifts and creative packages
  • A recommendation from someone you met a month ago
  • Calling a professor a connection without real two-way contact
  • Claiming a partner or endorser without documentation

The rules

What stays mine

  1. My voice. Every essay is drafted and refined in my own words. Tools can organize arguments and ask questions. They never write the final prose.
  2. My facts. Every date, service record, project outcome and transcript credit stays true and backed by proof.
  3. My call. Where to apply, what to study and how much history to share is my decision.

Save, move, reset

Progress lives in this browser on this device. Copy the backup code to keep it safe or to open the same progress somewhere else.

Dates and routes follow the November 2026 audit of each school's official transfer pages, linked on every card and on the clock. Standing dates can change for Fall 2027. Recheck the official page before you rely on any date, rate or aid rule.

Admit rates and GPA figures describe past cohorts at the school or program level. They are not personal odds. Faculty names are research anchors, not contacts or recommenders.

Personal background and dated academic planning figures appear only after unlocking the private strategy tools. Older research is labeled; the November 10 audit links current official sources. Verify final prompts and dates in your application.
